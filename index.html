HOW TO INTERPRET RESULTS

Trip generation: estimates person trips from population and assumed trip rates.

Trip distribution: uses a simplified gravity impedance function to represent interactions between zones.

Mode choice: responds to assumed fare, service frequency, attractiveness and policy adjustments.

Traffic assignment: applies an iterative congestion feedback approximation. It is not a validated full Frank–Wolfe implementation.

Road geometry: routes are requested from OSRM's public routing demo and follow routable OpenStreetMap roads. If no route is returned, that corridor is omitted rather than drawn as a straight line.

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Demonstration geography: Greater Banjul and selected national urban centres.

Potential data sources for a calibrated model: GBoS census and EA boundaries; validated household travel surveys; road centreline and condition inventory; traffic counts; GTFS or operator route and timetable data; terminal and stop locations; land-use and employment data.

OpenStreetMap basemap tiles require an internet connection. The model calculations run in this browser session.
